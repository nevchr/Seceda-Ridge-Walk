# Seceda V0.15 — regional surface pass

Foundation: committed V0.14 `e3c27b6`, preserved again at `3b9f606` before editing. Older portable builds and galleries remain available.

Direct aerial-photo ground colouring is replaced with interpreted natural-surface colour from the same licensed imagery. Geographic chromatic patterns are retained while photographed illumination and historical artificial footprints are suppressed. Terrain-shaped mineral exposure, fine material grain, limestone clusters and middle sward receive common scene sunlight. This is a regional transition pass, not another isolated showcase cliff.

The native survey, metre scale, EPSG:25832 coordinates, terrain detail selection, existing ridge/ledge shapes, route, props and collision are unchanged. The data audit records exact preservation checks. New fragments and vegetation are cosmetic authored detail, not additional measured terrain.

Walking remains the 327.98 m ridge route and connected southeast pasture in the same 0.602 km² envelope, subject to existing steep-bank and prop rejection. Leave the lower or middle trail to the southeast. Northern cliff faces, western escarpment, high massif and the outer 14 × 12 km terrain remain scenic. No walking bounds or valley infrastructure have been added.

Elevation is provincial DTM 2.5 m (CC0). Broad natural/artificial footprint masks use the historical provincial real-land-use map (CC0, 2001/2005). Geographic colour derives from Ortofoto 2023 RGB, Provincia Autonoma di Bolzano / Alto Adige, with AgEA, under CC BY 4.0. Source and modification details are in ASSETS.md and assets/terrain-v15/surface-albedo.json. Plants, individual fragments, erosion, materials and light are authored. Private reference photos remain excluded.

Rendered iteration evidence is in artifacts/surface-v015/WORKLOG.md. Final package validation and rendering costs are recorded in VALIDATION_V015.md.


The clearest changes are fuller modeled woodland across both valleys, low mapped shrub stands, finer scree/limestone transitions and a longer middle-sward blend. Shallow fractured exposures add relief to thin-soil shoulders; their basal vertices fit the survey and their raised vertices lift by at most 0.85 m. They exclude the main massif and a 55 m margin around walking bounds. The original elevation surface remains unchanged.

The ground-colour correction is approximate, not measured reflectance. Some baked-light/track traces can remain. Broad distant pasture still depends on material texture, repeated plant crowns remain apparent, and the generic limestone ingredients do not reconstruct every local geological feature. The outer landscape is scenic and its roads/buildings are not modeled infrastructure. No performance-driven detail reduction was made during this pass.
